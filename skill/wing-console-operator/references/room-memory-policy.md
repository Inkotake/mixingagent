# Room Memory Policy

## What Gets Remembered
- Room topology (devices, IPs, connections)
- Patch sheets (channel assignments, sources, phantom status)
- Band preferences (monitor mixes, EQ presets, FX choices)
- Incident history (past problems and solutions)
- Equipment inventory (mics, DIs, cables)

## Write Policy
- Tool-observed state: logged automatically
- User preference: requires explicit confirmation
- AI-inferred: flagged for review
- Safety rules: admin-only, cannot be changed by AI

## Privacy
- Room recordings, transcripts, band preferences may be sensitive
- Store locally when possible
- Document data retention policy
