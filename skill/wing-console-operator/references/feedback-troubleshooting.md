# Feedback Troubleshooting

## Quick Triage
1. Which mic? Which speaker?
2. Is it a specific frequency or broadband?
3. Does reducing monitor level stop it?

## Common Causes
- Monitor too loud for mic position
- Mic pointed at speaker (cardioid null should face speaker)
- EQ boost at resonant frequency
- Compression makeup gain too high
- Multiple open mics summing

## Safe Actions (in order)
1. Ask performer to move mic away from speaker
2. Reduce monitor send level for that mic
3. Identify and cut resonant EQ frequency
4. Last resort: reduce channel gain

## Unsafe Actions (avoid)
- Boosting EQ to "fix" feedback
- Adding compression to feedback channel
- Raising overall monitor level
