# Monitor Mix Workflows

## IEM / Floor Wedge Adjustment
1. Confirm performer and their bus assignment
2. Read current send levels from all channels to that bus
3. Ask performer what they need more/less of
4. Adjust one channel at a time, max 6dB change
5. Have performer confirm before next adjustment

## Common Requests
- "More of myself" → increase that channel's send to performer's bus
- "Less bass" → reduce bass channel send
- "More click" → increase click channel send
- "Everything is too quiet" → check bus master fader

## Safety
- Never increase overall bus level more than 3dB at once
- IEM volume too high can cause hearing damage
- Always read current state before adjusting
